# Business Profile rules, reviews, ranking

Tags: `[G]` re-fetched from the page named by its id in Sources (accessed 2026-10-02), `[H]` default,
`[U]` reported, not confirmed.

## Eligibility and identity

- `[G:gbp-guidelines]` A business must have a physical location customers can visit or travel to customers
  where they are. Content must be accurate and not prohibited.
- `[G:gbp-guidelines]` Name: the real-world name as used on storefront, website and stationery. Not allowed:
  marketing taglines, store codes, fully capitalised words, phone numbers, URLs, special characters without
  proof, location information, repeated bilingual names.
- `[G:gbp-guidelines]` Address: precise and accurate; P.O. boxes and virtual offices not acceptable;
  permanent fixed signage with the business name at the address.
- `[G:gbp-guidelines]` Service-area business: area not farther than about two hours' driving from the base;
  hide the address from customers when working from a residence.
- `[G:gbp-guidelines]` Hours: regular customer-facing hours. Categories: as few as possible, most specific.
  Description: services, products, mission and history; no low-quality or distracting text, promotions,
  links. Google may suspend access for violations.
- `[H]` Duplicate profiles for the same location, a changed name that no longer matches the storefront, and
  an address that differs between profile and website are the usual causes of suspension or lost visibility.

## Ranking

- `[G:local-ranking]` Relevance (profile versus query, completeness helps), distance (from the searcher),
  prominence (how well known; reviews, ratings, links and citations). Payment cannot improve local ranking.
- `[G:local-ranking]` Improve by verifying, keeping details current and complete, replying to reviews, adding
  photos and videos, and for shops showing in-store products.
- `[U]` Practitioners report the primary category as the field to get right first; a wrong primary category is the costliest
  mistake. The primary category is not tied to a fixed list here: read the category list in the profile.

## Reviews

- `[G:maps-policy]` Not allowed: incentives for posting or changing reviews, discouraging negative reviews,
  selectively soliciting positive ones, pressuring users to review on the premises. Allowed: asking for
  content that represents a genuine experience, without incentives.
- `[G:review-snippet]` Markup rules: see skill rule 9. The business cannot mark up reviews about itself to
  gain stars.
- `[H]` Review rhythm: a steady flow with replies from the owner within a few days. Treat a sudden burst
  of five-star reviews, near-identical wording or single-review accounts as signals to examine, flag at
  two or more together. This is a screening aid, not a verdict.
- `[H]` Reply rules: thank, be specific, never disclose customer data, never argue in public; for health
  and legal professions, do not confirm that the reviewer is a client.

## Verification and what to ask the client

- `[H]` Ask for: owner access to the profile, the list of services and areas, real hours with exceptions,
  photos taken on site, whether the address can be public.
- `[U]` Verification methods (postcard, phone, video) and the order Google offers them vary by business
  and country; confirm in the profile before telling the client what to expect.
- `[U]` The profile questions-and-answers API was reported discontinued on 2025-11-03; do not plan work
  on it.
