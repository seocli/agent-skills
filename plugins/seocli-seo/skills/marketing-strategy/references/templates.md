# Templates

Fill only with facts from the evidence pack; unknown = `n/d`. Client-facing copies are in Italian.

## Strategy document

```
# Strategy: <client> - <period>
1. Diagnosis: business model, margin, capacity, sales cycle; market and demand (volume, trend,
   seasonality); alternatives the customer really uses; current performance and measurement
   quality; binding constraint in one sentence; what is unknown.
2. Objective: north star (metric, baseline, target, date); guardrails (CAC or CPA cap, margin, capacity).
3. Customer: who, trigger, job to be done, objections; top 10 category entry points.
4. Positioning: alternatives, unique attributes, value and proof, category frame, one-line claim.
5. Guiding policy: what we will do and what we will NOT do.
6. Channel plan: channel | role (capture, creation, retention) | budget | outcome range | time to signal | owner.
7. Measurement plan: KPI tree, tracking requirements, attribution approach, planned tests.
8. Hypotheses: cards (`hypotheses.md`).
9. Roadmap: 30/60/90 days.
10. Risks and assumptions; what would change our mind.
11. Governance: cadence, owners, who approves what.
```
Quality bar: does it make a choice that could be wrong? Does it say what is not being done? If every
channel has equal priority it is a wish list.

## Quarterly plan

```
# Quarterly plan Qn YYYY - <client>
North star: <metric> from <baseline> to <target> (conservative / base / optimistic)
Budget: media, production, fees; split capture / creation / retention / test
Seasonality notes
Month 1 Foundation: tracking and consent fixes, business profile, baseline dashboards, launch
  highest-intent capture, start 1-2 hypotheses
Month 2 Build and test: content and landing pages, creative tests, one incrementality test,
  mid-quarter kill or scale decisions
Month 3 Scale and consolidate: reallocate 10-20% to the winner, refresh top pages, plan next
  quarter, review with the client
Hypotheses: H-01..H-0n   Leading indicators dashboard   Risks and mitigations
Decision gates: week 4 tracking ok? week 8 kill or scale; week 12 continue or redesign
Client actions required, with dates: access, approvals, assets, sales feedback
```

## Monthly report

```
1. Summary (one paragraph): result vs plan, the key decision.
2. Scorecard: north star + 3-5 inputs vs target and vs scenario band (provenance on each number).
3. Said vs happened: hypothesis table, confirmed / refuted / inconclusive.
4. What we now believe: learnings and updated assumptions.
5. Next month: 3 priorities, budget moves, tests, risks, client actions with dates.
6. Appendix: channel detail from the specialists.
```
Report variance to target and to the scenario band, not only trend. Compare with the same month last
year when seasonality matters.
