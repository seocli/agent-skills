# Italian market and compliance

Not legal advice. The strategist flags a risk and says: verify with the client's legal advisor or
data protection officer. Add a compliance gate ("who validates claims before launch") to the plan
for every regulated sector.

## Business landscape

- `[G]` Micro-firms (up to 9 employees) were over 4.2 million in 2022: 94.9% of firms, 27.2% of
  value added, 25.0% of investment and 42.3% of employment; large firms (250+) are 4,343 firms
  generating 34.5% of value added (S1). Note: the 2023 yearbook edition reports 2020 data (95.2%);
  cite the edition with its reference year.
- `[H]` Implications: owner decides, low digital maturity, budgets often a few hundred to a few
  thousand euro a month, little in-house marketing. Clusters: craftspeople, professional firms,
  food service, hospitality, retail, B2B manufacturing and export districts (multilingual, trade
  fairs, long cycles). North and South differ in digital adoption and purchasing power.

## Consumers and channels `[H]`

- Search is dominated by one engine; maps and business profile matter for local.
- Mobile usage is very high; reviews, word of mouth and trust marks carry weight; payment habits
  include cash on delivery, prepaid cards and instalment payments.
- WhatsApp is a default business channel; phone still matters for services.
- Marketplaces are major for e-commerce. Platform-reach figures change yearly: re-read a current
  digital-statistics report before quoting any figure to a client.
- Price sensitivity is high; promotions and shipping thresholds matter.
- Seasonality: August (Ferragosto) and late December are quiet for B2B; summer peaks tourism; Black
  Friday and Christmas for e-commerce; saldi in winter and summer (regional dates); Easter, Mother's
  Day, Father's Day (19 March), Valentine's Day. Do not judge a test in August.

## Consent and privacy

- `[G]` Garante cookie guidelines (June 2021, six months for sites to comply): scrolling is not
  valid consent unless part of a documented user action; cookie walls are generally prohibited
  unless equivalent content or service is available without consent; a declined choice is not
  re-presented unless conditions change, the site cannot tell whether a cookie is set, or at least
  six months passed; users can revoke any time; analytics cookies limited to statistics (S2).
- Marketing tracking and remarketing need consent; server-side delivery does not remove that need
  `[H]`. Consent Mode must be configured with a certified consent platform for EEA personalisation
  features `[H]`; check the `google-ads` skill for the current requirement.
- Forms: separate consent boxes for marketing and service, no pre-ticked boxes, keep consent
  records, handle data-subject requests. Email: soft opt-in for similar products to existing
  customers; telemarketing and the opposition register: verify `[H]`.
- Analytics and transfers outside the EU: the Garante ruled against a past analytics product in
  June 2022, and the EU-US framework changed the picture in 2023. The current position on the
  client's analytics tool is not verified here: verify with the client's legal advisor and say so.
- Client private data must not go to non-EU models (repo constitution VII).

## Advertising rules `[H]`, verify per case

- Misleading advertising and unfair commercial practices (Consumer Code, enforced by the competition
  authority, with significant fines); advertising must be recognisable as such (self-regulatory code
  and influencer disclosure rules); large-reach influencer guidelines: verify thresholds.
- Sector limits: health and nutrition claims, medical, dental, legal and other professional codes,
  gambling ad ban, alcohol, financial services, cosmetics, supplements.
- Promotions: lowest price in the prior 30 days must be shown for price reductions; prices to
  consumers are VAT-inclusive.
- Distance selling: 14-day withdrawal right, pre-contract information.
- Accessibility rules for e-commerce apply from June 2025 with exemptions for micro-enterprises
  providing services: verify scope.
- Platform category rules (credit, employment, housing, social issues, health data) live in the ads
  skills.

## Agency practice `[H]`

- Explain that the fee pays strategy and execution and the media is paid by the client directly.
- Invoicing is fattura elettronica (split payment for public bodies); monthly retainers are common;
  agree payment terms in writing (30, 60 or 90 days are common in B2B) and the cash effect.
- Grants and tax credits for advertising and digitalisation exist and change often: verify current
  availability before mentioning one.
- Local references and Italian case studies build trust; avoid jargon; free first consultations are
  normal.
