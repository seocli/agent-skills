# Agency client lifecycle

| Stage | Typical duration | Output | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-sales audit | 1-2 weeks | Quick audit, opportunity range, proposal | Strategist with SEO and Ads |
| Onboarding | 2-4 weeks | Access, tracking, baseline, strategy v1 | Strategist and account manager |
| Strategy | 1-2 weeks | Strategy document, quarterly plan | Strategist |
| Execution | rolling | Two-week sprints | Specialists |
| Monitoring | weekly | Alerts, anomaly checks | Specialists, strategist reviews |
| Monthly report | monthly | Report and decisions | Strategist |
| Quarterly review | quarterly | Plan update, reallocation, renewal case | Strategist and client |

Durations are `[H]`.

## Pre-sales audit (cheap, outside-in)

- Business: offer, price, differentiation, reviews, brand, sales process if shared.
- Visibility: organic rankings, Search Console if the prospect grants access, local pack, business
  profile completeness, competitor share of voice.
- Paid: competitor activity in the public ad libraries.
- Site: speed, mobile UX, CTA clarity, trust signals, tags present, consent banner compliance.
- Compliance: cookie banner, privacy policy, basics (see `italy.md`).
- Opportunity: search demand in Italy x realistic share x CVR x margin, as a range.

Deliverable: 3 findings, 3 opportunities with ranges, 1 risk, proposed scope, an honest "what we do
not know". Customised, never a generic free audit.

## Onboarding checklist

1. Access: analytics, Search Console, ads accounts, Merchant Center, tag manager, paid social
   business manager and pixel or server events, CMS, DNS if needed, CRM, call tracking.
2. Questionnaire: ideal customer, top customers, margins, capacity, seasonality, sales process, past
   campaigns, brand guidelines, competitors, legal constraints.
3. Tracking audit and fix: events, conversions, consent mode, offline conversions, UTM convention,
   CRM link.
4. Baselines: 12-24 months of traffic, leads and revenue by source, data gaps marked.
5. Success defined: north star, KPI tree, targets, review dates, and a shared definition of a
   qualified lead.
6. Governance: who approves, channels of communication (a WhatsApp group only with written rules),
   meeting cadence.
7. Quick wins for 30 days: business profile fixes, tracking fixes, obvious conversion fixes,
   negative keywords, review requests.

## Strategy, execution, monitoring

Strategy: diagnosis, objective, customer, positioning, guiding policy, channel plan, measurement
plan, hypotheses, 30/60/90 roadmap (`templates.md`). Execution: two-week sprints, one major change
per channel per test window. Monitoring: specialists watch pacing, tracking health and anomalies;
the strategist reads the exceptions, not every metric.

## Monthly report

Structure in `templates.md`. Rules: lead with decisions, explain variance causes, separate facts
from interpretation, 2-4 pages, Italian for the client, one reconciled view (never per-platform
totals added up).

## Churn signals and responses

- Client does not understand the report: simplify and walk through it verbally.
- Results lag what sales promised: reset expectations with data and leading indicators; never hide.
- Dependence on one person: document.
- Lead quality disputes: shared definition of a qualified lead and a monthly lead review with the
  client's sales side.
