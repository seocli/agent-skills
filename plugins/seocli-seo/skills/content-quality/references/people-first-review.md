# People-first review: Who/How/Why, E-E-A-T, YMYL, spam, AI content

Rules 1-11 in `SKILL.md` are the verified base. This file is the working checklist.

## Review order (stop at the first failed gate and say so)

1. **Right page, indexable?** Page type fits the SERP (`sxo-page-fit.md`); the page is indexed and can show a snippet.
2. **Why.** Would the page exist without search traffic? Signs it would not: templated sets, text padded
   to a length, headings stuffed with query variants.
3. **Who.** Visible byline linking to an author page; real, checkable credentials; About, Contact and
   a responsible entity. For shops: payment security, customer service, returns.
4. **How.** For reviews, comparisons and tests: how it was tested, with what, when. Disclose
   automation or AI where a reader would expect it; disclose affiliate and sponsored relationships.
5. **Unique value.** Compare with the top results: original data, photos, first-hand experience, a named
   expert review, a clear update date that matches real changes.
6. **Accuracy.** Facts checked; sources cited for claims that matter; no invented statistics.

## E-E-A-T proofs to look for (an evaluation framework, not a score)

- Experience: first-hand photos, screenshots, process, own measurements.
- Expertise: author with relevant background; content correct and complete for the topic.
- Authoritativeness: recognised as a go-to source (citations, mentions, official status).
- Trust: accurate, honest, safe; secure transactions; clear responsibility; consistent facts across the site.
Do not turn these into a numeric score for the client. If the client asks for one, say it is a
conversation aid, not a Google metric.

## YMYL

Ask first whether the topic can affect health, money, safety or society. If yes: demand named expert
authorship or review, primary sources, up-to-date dates, and a conservative tone. Raise the severity
of an unsupported claim by one level. Route legal and medical wording back to the client's professional.

## Spam policies used in content reviews

Screen for: scaled content, doorway pages, thin affiliate pages copying merchant text, expired-domain
repurposing, hidden text, keyword stuffing, third-party content hosted to borrow the site's ranking
(site reputation abuse; wire syndication, user-generated platforms and clearly authored columns are
described as not violating when handled properly). If pages cannot be improved, the page can be
excluded from indexing rather than left to dilute the site.

## Generative AI content

Allowed under the conditions in Rule 6. Provide the human-added-value checklist to the client:
first-hand data, original numbers, screenshots, a named expert review, an honest update date. Page
sets built by AI without per-page data are a scaled-content risk. Do not use tools that claim to detect or defeat detection.

## Recovery from a quality drop

Use `search-analytics` to separate update, technical and seasonal causes. Wait a week after completion
(Rule 12 there). Improve the whole weak area, not individual keywords; improvements can take months and
carry no guarantee (Rule 8).
